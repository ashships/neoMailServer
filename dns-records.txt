NeoMail DNS setup instructions — not a production zone import

Confirmed sender: your-sender@your-verified-domain.com.
Use your-worker-domain.com consistently for sender verification.

1. Worker custom domain
   Bind your chosen hostname (for example your-worker-domain.com) through Workers
   custom domain configuration. The route in wrangler.toml is commented until
   the operator confirms ownership and the spelling.

2. Sender verification
   Add the actual sender domain in each active provider dashboard. Copy its
   exact DKIM, verification, SPF/return-path and tracking records. Do not invent
   provider SPF includes or DKIM selectors. Provider regions and accounts vary.
   Most mail authentication CNAME records must be DNS-only; follow the provider.

3. SPF
   Maintain one SPF record per hostname. Merge only validated provider includes.
   Check the maximum ten DNS-triggering lookups; multiple broad includes can
   exceed it. Do not copy the old combined SPF record from earlier versions.

4. DMARC
   Add a record at _dmarc.<ACTUAL-SENDER-DOMAIN>. Start with a monitored policy
   and a real monitored report mailbox, then tighten after verifying legitimate
   SPF/DKIM alignment. Example ONLY (replace domain and reporting mailbox):
   v=DMARC1; p=none; rua=mailto:dmarc@YOUR-DOMAIN; adkim=r; aspf=r
   Move toward quarantine/reject after validating legitimate senders.

5. Optional inbound routing
   Configure the actual subdomain in Cloudflare Email Routing and route approved
   addresses to this Worker. Copy the exact MX/TXT records Cloudflare generates.
   Do not hard-code sample MX hosts or replace existing mail hosting blindly.
   Add the recipient to active Sender identities. Inbound raw messages above
   128 KiB are rejected. MIME/attachment parsing is not implemented.

6. Verification
   Check public DNS resolution and provider verification status. Send controlled
   tests and inspect Authentication-Results, SPF, DKIM, DMARC and delivery events.
   Authentication improves trust; it cannot guarantee inbox placement.
